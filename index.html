§1 Six acquisition pathways

· DoDI 5000.02 Change 2 (Apr 8, 2026) — tailorable, combinable

Programs no longer follow a single waterfall. Each pathway is purpose-built; PMs can combine them (e.g., MTA prototype → MCA production) with MDA approval. Click a card to expand.

§2 Cross-cutting functional areas

· apply to all pathways with tailoring

Each area has its own governing DoDI. Systems Engineering, T&E, Cybersecurity, and Life Cycle Sustainment are the four largest; cost estimating, mission engineering, IP, and HSI round out the policy stack.

§3 Requirements · the Joint Force Requirements Process

· replaces JCIDS · CJCSI 5123.01J (Jan 15, 2026)

JCIDS was disestablished by SecDef memo on Aug 20, 2025. Service-level requirements now belong to the services. Joint problems flow through a small new machinery: KOPs → RRAB → JAR funding, with MEIA tying industry experimentation back in.

§4 Technical reviews — the SE event chain

· applies to MCA pathway · DoDI 5000.88

Twelve formal reviews structure technical progress in the Major Capability pathway. Each gates a baseline (Functional, Allocated, or Product) or readiness state. Tailored heavily for other pathways.

§5 Milestone document matrix

· MCA pathway · per DoDI 5000.85 & 5000.02 Change 2

What's statutorily or regulatorily required at each MCA decision point. MDAs can tailor, but statutory requirements (marked) cannot be omitted without waiver.

§6 Contract types & phase mapping

· FAR Part 16 · risk shifts left to right

Contract type matches risk: cost-reimbursement for ambiguous scope (development), fixed-price as designs stabilize (production). Two views below — type characteristics, and typical type by acquisition phase.

Contract type characteristics

Likely contract type by MCA phase

As designs mature and risk drops, contracts move from cost-reimbursement (CPFF/CPIF/CPAF) toward fixed-price (FPI(F)/FFP). Software pathway typically uses CPIF or IDIQ with task orders; Services pathway favors PBSA.

§7 Cost estimating methods

· DoDI 5000.73 · used at every milestone for MDAPs

Estimating precision improves as the program matures and data accumulates. Early phases lean on analogy and parametrics; late phases use actuals.

Required cost artifacts for MDAPs at every milestone: CARD (description), CCE (component estimate), CCP (component position), ICE (independent estimate, by CAPE).

§9 PPBE — the annual resource cycle

· to be replaced by Defense Resourcing System per PPBE Reform Commission

Programs are event-driven; budgets are calendar-driven. PPBE forces them to align. Four phases — Planning, Programming, Budgeting, Execution — span ~21 months from strategic guidance to the President's Budget submission to Congress.

Reform in progress. The Congressional PPBE Reform Commission's 2024 final report recommended replacing PPBE with the Defense Resourcing System (DRS) — three phases (Strategy → Resource Allocation → Execution) with continuous analysis. 26 of 28 recommendations are in DoD's implementation plan as of January 2025; full transition expected over several budget cycles. The current PPBE structure remains operative for now.

Naming

Under EO 14347 (Sept 5, 2025), "Department of War" is authorized as a secondary designation for the Department of Defense, "Secretary of War" for SECDEF, etc. The statutory name remains "Department of Defense"; only Congress can change it. Official documents and the comptroller's website (war.gov) increasingly use the secondary name; CRS and other sources flag both.
